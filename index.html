What this is

Honestly Happened makes short animated videos about real moments from history that sound made up: a town that moved its church down the road, an emperor who declared war on the sea. Every story is checked against sources before it's made. True ones end with "Honestly." Legends end with "Allegedly."

The posting tool

The channel's videos are scheduled with a small private tool, "Honestly Happened Poster", run by the channel owner. It connects only to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts, to upload videos and read their statistics. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.
